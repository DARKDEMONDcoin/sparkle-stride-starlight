# PLAYBOOK: DIAGNOSTIC PATTERNS (load on every analysis)
Use these "symptom, likely cause, check, proposal" patterns, always after the baseline review:
1. Many campaigns, each with about 8 to 12 conversions a month, none leaving learning. Cause: over-segmentation. Check conversions per campaign and overlap. Propose a staged consolidation, highest spend first. [E]
2. Meta account running traffic, lead, video-view, engagement, and retargeting campaigns at once, with rising CPA and falling new customers. Cause: fragmented signal and wrong objectives. Check whether conversion campaigns are running, 90-day results, the creative pipeline, and the post-click experience. Propose stripping to one or two sales/conversion ad sets with budget adjustments, moving the best creative into the Advantage+ structure, and testing a purchase call to action against lead-style offers. A reported case recovered from roughly 200 to 300 new customers a month to about 900, with CPA from above $30 to $13 to $21. [E]
3. PMax or Smart Bidding "isn't working" after a few weeks with very few conversions. Cause: not enough signal or an aggressive target, or immature data. Check conversion volume, goal choice, target level, lag profile, and total sales for the advertised products. Propose a lower-friction conversion goal (micro-conversion ladder), removing or relaxing the target, and waiting for volume before judging. A reported case showed 2 then 6 attributed purchases while total sales of the products rose sharply, and platform results caught up by month three. [E]
4. Recent performance looks terrible but the account was stable. Cause: conversion lag (immature data), a seasonal swing, or a broken tag. Check the lag profile, year-over-year, change history, and tracking. Propose waiting or fixing tracking, not cutting. [G][E]
5. Lead gen: many leads but sales says they're junk. Cause: optimizing form fills without offline feedback; optional causes include Search Partner/placement mix, demographic skew, and a weak page. Check lead goals, offline feedback, age or placement distribution, and the landing page. Propose qualified-lead goals, daily offline uploads, lead verification, exclusions on evidence, and a better-qualifying form. [G][E]
6. Search CPCs rose after launching Performance Max. Cause: brand cannibalization or overlap. Check brand share and search-term overlap. Propose brand exclusions, campaign-level negatives, and clear Search/PMax boundaries. [E]
7. PMax has several asset groups with identical assets. Cause: signal duplication. Propose consolidating or truly differentiating the assets. [E]
8. Meta ads: cost per result roughly doubled versus the past. Cause: creative fatigue or audience saturation. Check ad-level trends, concept diversity, and restrictions (small audience, narrow placements, few ads). Propose new distinct concepts and formats, wider targeting and placements. [M][E]
9. High spend, low in-platform ROAS, but business is growing. Cause: attribution gaps. Check blended and business-level results before cutting. [E]
10. Traffic is fine, conversion rate is low. Cause: landing page, offer, or checkout. Check message match, speed, mobile experience, form, and the click-to-view ratio. Propose page fixes before more ad spend. [E]
11. Many clicks but very few landing page views (Meta). Cause: slow page, broken link, or tracking gap. Check speed, the link, and the pixel. Propose fixes before touching creative. [V]
12. One landing page receives traffic from several different ad angles and converts poorly. Cause: message mismatch. Propose separate pages (page briefs) per angle, or realign ads to the page. [V]
13. Small budget with AI Max or PMax selected. Cause: not enough data for learning. Propose Search with tight geography and high-intent keywords, simple bidding, and honest expectations. [E]
14. A recent change "worked" after two days. Cause: noise or immature data. Wait for the review date, then evaluate like-for-like. [E][D]
